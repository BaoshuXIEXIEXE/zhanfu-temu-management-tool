# TEMU US operations scope

Beyond message triage, support these operating areas:

- Products: find listings and SKUs, verify on-sale/off-sale state, diagnose suppression, check attributes, images, titles, variants, pricing, and compliance; prepare listing, edit, delist, or relist actions for approval.
- Orders and fulfillment: locate orders, extract shipment deadlines, address changes, tracking, carrier, cancellation, late-delivery and violation status; prepare fulfillment or appeal actions.
- Customer service and after-sales: locate tickets, refunds, returns, chargebacks, reviews, and complaints; match evidence and draft responses. External submissions require approval.
- Inventory: inspect sellable, reserved, frozen, inbound, returned, and low-stock conditions; estimate stockout and fulfillment risk.
- Ads and traffic: compare spend, sales, ROAS, impressions, clicks, conversion, and product-level traffic over useful periods; flag material changes and prepare bid/budget recommendations. Do not change campaigns without approval.
- Performance and finance: monitor sales, cancellation/refund rates, shipment and arrival timeliness, penalties, settlements, deductions, and account-fund restrictions; reconcile anomalies to orders where possible.
- Reviews and product quality: summarize new reviews, identify recurring defects or fit issues, connect them to SKUs, and propose listing, supplier, packaging, or quality-control improvements.
- Compliance and account health: inspect violations, IP complaints, certifications, policy deadlines, sales restrictions, and appeal status; retain evidence and escalate active risk.

Prefer comparisons and exceptions over raw totals. Separate observations from recommendations, and recommendations from approved execution.
